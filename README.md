# ClinicQ — Clinic Appointment and Live Queue System

## Project Overview

ClinicQ is a web-based Clinic Appointment and Live Queue Management System designed to simplify appointment scheduling and improve patient flow in clinics. It supports online appointment booking, patient check-in, live queue tracking, and clinic staff operations.

## Project Objectives

- Simplify the process of booking clinic appointments.
- Allow patients to check their appointment and queue status.
- Help receptionists manage appointments, walk-in patients, and queues.
- Help doctors manage their schedules and consultations.
- Provide clinic administrators with tools for managing staff, clinic rules, and reports.

## Main Features

- Patient registration and profile management
- Doctor search and appointment booking
- Appointment cancellation and rescheduling
- Patient check-in and live queue tracking
- Receptionist-assisted appointment booking
- Walk-in patient registration and queue management
- Doctor schedule and availability management
- Consultation notes and prescription recording
- Staff and clinic administration
- Reports and audit log management
